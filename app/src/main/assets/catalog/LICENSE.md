# Open recipe catalog and photographs

Recipe text from Vietnamese Wikibooks and three Vietnamese adaptations of English Wikibooks recipes is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Each record in recipes.json retains its source revision permalink, author/history link,
license URL and modifications. Original contributors are credited as the relevant
Wikibooks contributor community. Three English-source records are translated and
edited by MyRecipeLab; no claim that these are the only authentic variants is made.

Photographs have their own individual licenses, NOT automatically the text license.
For each reused Wikimedia Commons file, recipes.json and photo_credits.json retain
the creator, file description page, license and resize/conversion note. Some photos
illustrate a dish family; imageMatch explicitly states this. Thumbnails are resized
and converted to JPEG for offline display. No unrelated stock image is labeled as
a verified photograph of a specific recipe.

The app displays attribution and links in recipe details. When redistributing this
dataset, retain this notice and the per-record/image attribution and license data.
